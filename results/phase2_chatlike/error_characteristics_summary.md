**Reading this table**: rows 1–7 are Phase 1, frozen, TEST/DEV as noted per row. Rows 8–9 are new Phase 2 observations that refine rather than contradict rows 1 and 3 — they suggest مجاز مرسل's flatness (row 1) may be less "the model can't see it" and more "the strict-JSON format forecloses the reasoning that would find it, and even when reasoning is allowed, it doesn't converge reliably" (row 8), and that annotator-vs-model disagreement (row 9) is a distinct, previously invisible failure mode-worth discussing with Dr. Hussein specifically, since it may reflect genuine rhetorical ambiguity rather than model error.
