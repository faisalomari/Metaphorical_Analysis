## 2. Claims I was unsure about, and how I resolved them

- **"Limitations of REI" as a separate 2023 citation** (proposal reference #26): I could not find this as an independent publication distinct from Abd Alhadi, Hussein and Kuflik (2023) itself. I treated it as a likely duplicate/shorthand reference to the same paper and excluded it as a separate Works Cited entry (`00_proposal_reuse_map.md` part (d)). This is a judgment call under time pressure, not a certainty — if a second, genuinely independent "Limitations of REI" publication exists, it was not located by the searches run in this session.
- **Ali & Ahmad (2015), "Classical Arabic rhetoric," Journal of Arabic Literature** (proposal reference #14): I concluded this is very likely a garbled duplicate of the proposal's own, separately-listed and verifiable Hussein (2015) book citation (#27 in the proposal's list), given the matching year and overlapping subject, but I did not find positive confirmation that the Journal-of-Arabic-Literature version does not also exist independently — only that a direct search did not surface it. Treated as unverifiable and dropped, per the task's "do not add anything unverifiable" instruction, erring toward exclusion rather than inclusion.
- **Whether `v6` or `reply1-TK` is truly the "latest" proposal version**: filenames alone are ambiguous (`reply1-TK` suggests a reviewer-response/track-changes pass, which could read as later than a plain `v6`). I resolved this using the embedded OOXML `dcterms:modified` timestamps (v6: 2026-06-10T12:18, six minutes after reply1-TK's 2026-06-10T12:12), which is a real but thin signal — both saves are six minutes apart on the same day, and the only content difference between them is the title/subtitle line, so this choice has no material effect on the reuse-map findings regardless.
- **Whether Phase 3's GPT-5.4-mini cross-model re-score and the original Gemini-only screen should be presented as one table or two in `04_results.md`**: I presented the span-level, two-model re-score (`results/phase3_crossmodel/summary.md`) as the primary table, since it is the more rigorous and more recent scoring, and only mentioned the original verse-level, Gemini-only numbers in prose for the handful of subtypes where the task instructions specifically asked for them (كناية لغوية, استعارة تمثيلية). This is a presentation choice, not a numeric judgment call — both sets of numbers are real and both are cited to their actual source files.

## 3. Proposal content that conflicts with current results

This is covered in full in `00_proposal_reuse_map.md`, which is the primary deliverable for this question. The headline conflict, repeated here because it is easy to miss on a skim of the paper-draft files alone: **the proposal and the executed thesis are not the same research project.** The proposal's entire method (rule-based explicit-tashbīh detection → AraBERT fine-tuning → planned manual transformation of 29,265 poems into implicit-simile training data, reporting 95.71%/95.46%/95.99% accuracy/precision/recall on a binary classification task) does not appear anywhere in the executed thesis, which instead zero-shot/few-shot prompts general-purpose LLMs for span-level, multi-element identification across four elements (only one of which, simile, the proposal's own method directly addressed, and simile is not where the executed project's difficulty findings concentrate). None of the proposal's four sub-research-questions (RQ1.1–1.4) survived into the executed thesis's actual RQs except the general spirit of RQ1.4. This is not a minor drift in scope; it is a change of research program, and the new paper's introduction and methods sections (`01_introduction.md`, `03_data_and_methods.md`) were written entirely from the executed project's own files, not adapted from the proposal's prose, for exactly this reason.

## 4. What is missing

- **A second, independent annotator.** Even once the blind re-annotation above is complete, it measures *intra*-annotator (self-)agreement, not independent inter-annotator agreement, since the only person with the relevant subject-matter expertise on this project is Prof. Hussein himself. Whether to pursue a genuinely independent second annotator remains an open, unresolved decision for the supervisors (`PROJECT_STATE.md` Phase 2 §4 item 5), not something this task could resolve or default an answer for.
- **Claude Sonnet 4.6 on the Phase 3 subtype screen.** Only Gemini 3.5 Flash (the original screen) and GPT-5.4-mini (the later cross-model re-score) have been run on the twenty-five-subtype task; Claude was never run on it (estimated at $4.96–5.64 if added, per `results/phase3_crossmodel/cost_estimate.md`, not approved or run in this task, consistent with "make no API calls"). Every Phase 3 cross-model claim in `04_results.md` is explicitly scoped as two-model, not three-model, for this reason.
- **Negative sampling for Phase 3.** The entire twenty-five-subtype screen is recall-only by design; a negative-sampling follow-up that would make precision/F1 computable for at least the most-discussed subtypes (استعارة تمثيلية, كناية لغوية) is designed in the source documents but not run.
- **Config C's full-poem Nabigha run.** Only a narrow, known-positive-only probe exists for a second poem; Config C's one real F1 figure (0.316 strict, Imru2) remains single-poem.
- **A properly-scored explanation-augmented prompt test.** Phase 1's illustrative explanation-sample run (§3i of `PROJECT_STATE.md`) was one run per model, never scored against ground truth for correctness of the new positive calls it produced — only whether decisions flipped. This is not referenced in `04_results.md` since it is illustrative only and the task's instructions scope Results to the four deliverables already covered; flagged here as a known gap if a future draft wants to report it.
- **Licensing/rights status of the ground-truth corpus.** Unresolved per `writing/paper_plan/reviewer_risks.md` §7 and `writing/paper_plan/TOP_5_BLOCKERS.md` item 1 — blocks any Data Availability statement. Not addressed in any of this task's five files, since it requires an answer from the supervisor/Prof. Hussein, not something derivable from existing project files.

## 5. What the Results section cannot yet claim

- **No claim beyond "periphrasis is reachable but not yet reliably decidable."** `04_results.md`'s reconciliation of Phase 1 and Phase 2 deliberately stops short of claiming periphrasis detection is "solved" by free-form reasoning — Config C's own run-to-run instability (most known-positive hits from only one of two runs) is reported as part of the finding, not a footnote to an otherwise-positive result, per `report_3i.md` §9's own framing ("reachable but not reliably decided").
- **No claim that the twenty-five-subtype screen's numbers are comparable to the four-element TEST table.** They differ in scoring design (recall-only vs. full P/R/F1), model roster (two models vs. three), and data pooling (DEV+TEST pooled vs. TEST-only) — `04_results.md` states each of these differences explicitly rather than letting a reader infer comparability from adjacent tables.
- **No claim that prompt-sensitivity "explains away" استعارة تمثيلية's or كناية لغوية's difficulty.** The Results section frames these as evidence of *instability* across models/prompts/example-count, which is itself a reportable finding, but stops short of claiming either subtype is actually easy for some undiscovered configuration — that would be an overclaim the two-model, mostly-single-run evidence does not support.
- **No claim about generalization beyond this five-poem, pre-Islamic/early-Islamic-period corpus.** Nothing in `04_results.md` states or implies that the element difficulty ordering, the subtype findings, or the model comparisons generalize to Andalusian, Abbasid-era ornate, or Mamluk-era poetry — this scope limit belongs to the (not-yet-drafted) Limitations section, and `03_data_and_methods.md`/`04_results.md` were written to avoid language that would read as a broader claim than the five-poem corpus supports.
- **No claim that the TEST-leakage-via-worked-examples finding has been quantified.** `03_data_and_methods.md` states the finding (three of four-or-five worked examples per element file are literal TEST-poem verses) and that its practical impact on the frozen numbers is "likely small but non-zero and unquantified" (the source document's own hedge, `example_gap_analysis.md` §0) — it does not, and should not, convert this into an estimated effect size, since no experiment has isolated one.
