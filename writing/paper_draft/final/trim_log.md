## What was cut, and why, section by section

**Abstract / Teaser / Keywords.** Cut the abstract's own meta-line reporting its word count ("Word count: approximately 215 words..."), which is drafting scaffolding, not article content, and the keyword section's discursive explanation of *why* the DHQ-controlled-vocabulary and project-keyword fields are unresolved (shortened to a single bracketed note each). No wording of the abstract or teaser prose itself, and no number inside either, was touched.

**Introduction.** Cut the paragraph-length in-text literature review of Kühn, Mitrović and Granitzer (2024), Attia et al. (2026), and Al Kaabi et al. (2024) down to a single sentence with the same three citations, since Background and Related Work covers all three in full and the Introduction's fuller version was pure duplication of that section, not new information. Tightened phrasing elsewhere (e.g. "Recognizing an instance of one of these devices in a line of verse" → "Recognizing an instance of one of these devices") without cutting any claim, number, or the RQ1–RQ3 statements, which are reproduced in full and unchanged.

**Background and Related Work — the largest cut, as the task instructed.** Four specific cuts account for most of the 765 words removed:
1. The opening paragraph's full restatement of the al-Jurjānī/al-Sakkākī historical framing (already given in full in the Introduction) was collapsed to a one-clause pointer ("the *maʿānī*/*bayān*/*badīʿ* scholastic tradition introduced above") plus the Cachia/Harb/Fontana citation, instead of repeating the same history a second time.
2. The "Foundational and theoretical references" subsection (Lakoff and Johnson's Conceptual Metaphor Theory, held "in reserve... should the discussion of استعارة require one," plus Cohen 1960 and Hayes and Krippendorff 2007, held in reserve for whenever inter-annotator-agreement figures exist) was cut entirely. Cohen (1960) and Hayes and Krippendorff (2007) are not dropped from the paper — they are now cited directly in Data and Methods, at the one place the text actually names "Cohen's κ and Krippendorff's α" as the planned agreement statistics, which is a more defensible citation placement than holding them unused in Related Work. Lakoff and Johnson (1980) is dropped from the Works Cited entirely: it was never actually invoked to interpret any result in Discussion or elsewhere, only flagged as available "if" needed — exactly the kind of unused reserve citation `00_merged_references.md`'s own "Open items" §2 already flagged as a DHQ reviewer risk if it stayed unused at final draft. 3.
